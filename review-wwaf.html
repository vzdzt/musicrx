When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?

When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?: Billie Eilish’s Eerie Dreamscape


Released March 29, 2019 via Interscope and Darkroom, Billie Eilish’s When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?—a 14-track, 41-minute dark pop plunge—hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 313,000 first-week units. Co-produced with Finneas O’Connell, it’s 6× Platinum by 2025 with 6.2 billion streams, a haunting triumph post-“Ocean Eyes.” Its dark green shadows linger in 2025’s zeitgeist. Veazy’s take: it’s a whispered nightmare, raw and captivating.


The Messaging and Theme


Billie’s inky-eyed stare, shot by Craig McDean, cloaks When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? in dread and surreal youth. “bury a friend” hisses “Why don’t you run from me?”—a monster’s taunt—while “when the party’s over” aches “I’ll only hurt you if you let me.” “bad guy” struts “I’m the bad guy, duh,” and “all the good girls go to hell” grins “My Lucifer is lonely.” At 16, Billie channels Gen Z’s angst—death, identity, online haze—into a dark green dreamscape X fans in 2025 call “timelessly unhinged.”


Production and Sound


Finneas O’Connell shapes When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? in their Highland Park bedroom, weaving lo-fi pop, trap bass, and eerie synths. “bad guy” thumps with a dental drill snap, “bury a friend” glitches with knife-sharpening creaks, and “xanny” hums through a fan-blown tube. Sparse yet potent, its Logic Pro layers birthed 2020s acts like Clairo and Phoebe Bridgers, a dark green echo in 2025.


Features


When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? thrives on Billie and Finneas’s alchemy, with a lone nod to Donald Glover’s sampled laugh in “bad guy.” Billie’s whispers haunt “ilomilo”—a game-inspired lullaby—and “my strange addiction” samples *The Office.* No big guests, just their dark green intimacy, pure and unshaken.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “bad guy,” “when the party’s over”—Catchy yet eerie, these dark green hooks pull you into Billie’s world effortlessly.

For The Dive: “bury a friend,” “xanny”—Glitchy depths and smoky haze reveal Billie’s dark green soul, raw and unfiltered.

Hidden Gems: “ilomilo,” “listen before i go”—Soft, spectral layers glow dark green, subtle treasures for patient ears.

Latest Obsessions: “bad guy (Live 2025)”—A March 2025 Coachella set, viral on X, recharges this dark green banger live.


Previous Works


Billie’s “Ocean Eyes” (2016) whispered her arrival, followed by dont smile at me (2017) with “Bellyache.” When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (2019) crystallized her dark green mystique, paving the way for Happier Than Ever (2021) and Hit Me Hard and Soft (2024).

Ratings

Overall Rating (84/100): 8.4/10

When We All Fall Asleep is Billie’s dark green whisper—a 41-minute nightmare of youth’s unease that grips tight. Its 6.2 billion streams and 2025 Coachella buzz prove its pull. Veazy’s hooked: eerie, bold, and fragile, it’s a spectral gem echoing in 2025’s quiet chaos.
