DAYTONA: Pusha T’s Ruthless Blade of Authenticity


Dropped on May 25, 2018 under G.O.O.D. Music and Def Jam, Pusha T’s DAYTONA—a 7-track, 21-minute gem—fully produced by Kanye West, hit #3 on the Billboard 200. With 77,000 first-week sales and 1.8 billion streams by March 16, 2025, it’s Platinum-certified hip-hop fire. Veazy’s 2025 cut: a green-sharp classic.


The Messaging and Theme


DAYTONA’s cover—Whitney Houston’s bathroom, snagged by Kanye for $85,000—drops you into Pusha’s dope-lord sermon. “If You Know You Know” flexes insider code, “Infrared” stabs “Your ghostwriters get to exist,” reigniting Drake beef X still hashes in 2025 (@36966_). It’s street royalty unbowed—green-sharp and unyielding.


Production and Sound


Kanye West sculpts DAYTONA in Jackson Hole, fusing soul samples with brutal drums. “The Games We Play” loops Jimmy Douglass, “Come Back Baby” twists The Mighty Hannibal into trap dust, all sharpened by Mike Dean. It’s lean chaos shaping 2025’s raw edge—green-sharp perfection.


Features


DAYTONA leans light—Rick Ross growls “Rolexes in the safe” on “Hard Piano,” Kanye West snaps on “What Would Meek Do?” with Meek Mill nods. They fuel Pusha’s green-sharp throne, never stealing it.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “If You Know You Know”, “Infrared”— Razor bars and booming beats hook fast, Pusha’s green-sharp street gospel welcoming rookies with venom.

For The Dive: “Come Back Baby”, “Santeria”— Soul chops and layered daggers dig deep, Pusha’s green-sharp soul baring scars for seekers.

Hidden Gems: “The Games We Play”, “Hard Piano”— Sleek swagger and gritty flexes glow, Pusha’s green-sharp craft shining beyond the beef.

Latest Obsessions: “Infrared (Live 2025)”— Pusha’s green-sharp Coachella 2025 sting reignites Drake talk—X (@36966_) buzzing, Veazy’s looping it.


Previous Works


Clipse’s Lord Willin’ (2002) and Hell Hath No Fury (2006) with Malice and The Neptunes, then My Name Is My Name (2013) and Darkest Before Dawn (2015)—DAYTONA hones Pusha’s green-sharp blade, echoed in It’s Almost Dry (2022).
