YEAT

Rage Rap's Enigmatic Trendsetter


From SoundCloud obscurity to TikTok ubiquity, Noah Olivier Smith, better known as Yeat, has emerged as one of the most distinctive voices in contemporary rap, crafting a hazy, psychedelic sound that's become the soundtrack to Gen Z's digital experience.


Rise to Fame


Yeat's ascendancy began with his viral 2021 single "Sorry Bout That", which spread like wildfire across TikTok platforms. The Portland-born rapper's hypnotic melodies, auto-tuned vocals, and surrealist slang quickly carved out a unique sound in the crowded trap landscape. His breakthrough mixtape Up 2 Më catapulted him to the forefront of the "rage rap" subgenre, with tracks like "Gët Busy" and "Monëy So Big" becoming anthems for a new wave of digitally-native rap fans. Signing to Field Trip Recordings, a partnership with Interscope Records, Yeat's rise from internet phenomenon to commercial success has been meteoric.


Career Milestones

  • 2021: Released breakthrough mixtape Up 2 Më, which gained immense popularity online

  • 2022: Released 2 Alivë, his commercial debut album, which peaked at #6 on the Billboard 200

  • 2022: "Rich Minion" featured in the Minions: The Rise of Gru promotional campaign

  • 2023: AfterLyfe debuted at #4 on the Billboard 200, his highest chart position to date

  • 2024: Lyfë 2 continued his commercial success, establishing him as a streaming powerhouse

Musical Style & Impact


Yeat's music presents a psychedelic take on trap, characterized by distorted bass, ethereal synths, and a unique vocabulary of idiosyncratic slang. Terms like "tonka", "twizzy", and his signature "lüh" prefix have become embedded in rap vernacular. His production style, often helmed by producers like Trgc and Supah, features aggressive, video game-like soundscapes that have defined the "rage" aesthetic. Beyond music, Yeat's influence extends to fashion, with his signature turban becoming an iconic visual element of his brand.


Essential Listening

For Newcomers: "Monëy So Big," "Sorry Bout That," "Gët Busy," "Poppin"

For The Dive: "Turban," "Still Countin," "Flawless," "Talk"

Hidden Gems: "Kant Changë," "Rokstar," "Lying 4 Fun," "X ta C"


Legacy


Though still in the early stages of his career, Yeat has already left an indelible mark on rap's sonic landscape. His ability to craft addictive melodies wrapped in surrealist production has influenced a generation of emerging artists. As a bridge between the SoundCloud rap era and the TikTok-driven music discovery of today, Yeat represents a new paradigm of artist development in the digital age. With each release expanding his sonic palette while maintaining his distinctive style, Yeat continues to push the boundaries of what trap music can sound like in the 2020s.
