Introduction

Released February 15 2019 independently, Lucki’s Freewave 3 is a 15 track 30 minute cloud rap mixtape. With 1.2 billion streams by May 2025 (estimated), it’s a cult favorite. Producers ChaseTheMoney and Earl Sweatshirt craft its hazy vibe. Chronicling Lucki’s move from Chicago to NYC, it’s a raw diary of addiction and heartbreak, cementing his underground legacy.

Messaging and Theme

Freewave 3 is a stark confessional of addiction and lost love. “Peach Dream” reveals “Got my mama googling lean,” a plea from family. “More Than Ever” admits “I know she ain’t loyal, but she make me better,” blurring drugs and romance. Lucki’s monotone delivery masks pain, crafting a hazy narrative of self destruction and fleeting hope that resonates in 2025.

Production

ChaseTheMoney, Earl Sweatshirt, and others shape Freewave 3’s hypnotic sound. “All In” pulses with Earl’s lo fi grit, while “Politics” floats on minimalist pads. “Peach Dream”’s spacey keys evoke a drugged out haze. Lucki’s laid back flow glides over trap beats, though some tracks feel repetitive. The 30 minute runtime keeps its moody coherence, a cloud rap gem.

Features

Freewave 3 is a solo affair, with no guest verses. Lucki’s voice carries the weight, his nasally monotone weaving tales of addiction and heartbreak. Producers like Earl Sweatshirt on “All In” and ChaseTheMoney add sonic depth, but Lucki’s raw introspection dominates, a choice that amplifies the mixtape’s personal, hazy intensity.

Previous Works

Lucki debuted with Alternative Trap 2013, followed by Body High 2014 and Freewave 2015. Watch My Back 2017 honed his confessional style. Freewave 3 refines this with tighter production, leading to Days B4 III 2019 and Flawless Like Me 2022, which hit number 12 on Billboard 200. Its hazy sound remains a fan favorite in 2025.

Legacy & Reception

With a 79/100 critic score on Album of the Year, Freewave 3 is hailed as “Lucki’s magnum opus.” Its 1.2 billion streams by May 2025 (estimated) and cult following underscore its impact. “Peach Dream” and “All In” remain underground staples, with fans on X in 2025 calling it a “trap classic.”

Awards Recognition

Freewave 3 earned no major award nominations but garnered critical praise and a Must Hear badge on Album of the Year. Its 1.2 billion streams by 2025 (estimated) and influence on cloud rap highlight its underground legacy.
