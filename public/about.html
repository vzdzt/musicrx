About MusicRx

March 22, 2025

MusicRx is more than a site—it’s a sonic sanctuary born in 2025, dissecting the chaos and beauty of music with razor-sharp analysis.

We’re obsessed with the art form—spanning punk-trap hybrids to glitchy soundscapes—delivering reviews, news, and takes that hit like a bass drop.

How We Started

MusicRx kicked off in a basement studio, sparked by a late-night debate over Carti’s MUSIC drop in January 2025.

Three friends, fueled by energy drinks and a shared hatred of shallow reviews, built this from scratch—no funding, just passion.

Two months later, we’re here—hundreds of readers deep, chasing the high of music that actually means something.
