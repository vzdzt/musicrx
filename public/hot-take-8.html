Festivals Lost Their Soul

Hot Take #8 | March 8, 2025 | By MusicRx Staff
Rolling Loud California 2025 GA: $179 | VIP: $499

A car payment for GA tickets? That's the new normal at Rolling Loud. VIP packages hitting $499 are straight-up "flexing for the 'Gram" territory.

Coachella 2025 GA: $549

Dropping half a stack to stand in a desert with influencers who think "vibes" is a personality. The math ain't mathing.

"Most years, these fests serve up sets so mid, you're wondering if you paid for a Spotify playlist with worse sound quality."

But 2025 threw us a curveball. Travis Scott at Coachella was pure apocalyptic energy—pyro blasting, sci-fi stage design, and "FE!N" creating human tsunamis. Tyler, The Creator proved he's a festival cheat code, delivering a masterclass in performance art.

The real MVP? Playboi Carti at Rolling Loud. MUSIC's "Radar" channeled 2006 Atlanta vibes, complete with a Gucci Mane-level energy and The Weeknd cameo. That Pelle Pelle drip? Pure 2000s streetwear revival magic.

"Small venues—your local 500-cap rooms—are out here delivering raw, sweaty, no-filter shows for a fraction of the cost. That's where the soul of music lives."

But let's keep it real: these moments are rare gems in a sea of mediocrity. Most festival sets have you dodging mud pits while some TikTok hypeman gives you a 30-minute performance of their one viral hit.

The corporate cash grab is real, and these prices? Straight disrespectful. While the big festivals luck out occasionally with legendary performances from artists like Travis, Tyler, and Carti, the soul of live music still beats strongest in those intimate venue shows.

Verdict for 2025's festival season: Strong 7 to light 8. The highs were stratospheric, but the price tags and overall experience still need serious work. Keep supporting your local venues—that's where the real magic happens.
