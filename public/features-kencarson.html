KEN CARSON

Opium's Rage Rap Pioneer


From SoundCloud origins to Playboi Carti's Opium label star, Ken Carson has emerged as one of the most distinctive voices in rage rap, blending distorted production with melodic sensibilities to create a sound that's both chaotic and infectious.


Rise to Fame


Born Kenyatta Frazier on April 11, 2000, in Atlanta, Georgia, Ken Carson initially gained a following through SoundCloud releases and his association with Playboi Carti. After signing to Carti's Opium label in 2019, Carson began building momentum with singles like "Yale" and "Butterfly". His first major project Project X (2021) established his hypnotic, high-energy style, but it was X (2022) that truly broke him into the mainstream, followed by A Great Chaos (2023) cementing his position in the rage rap scene.


Career Milestones

  • 2019: Signed to Playboi Carti's Opium label

  • 2021: Released breakout mixtape Project X, introducing his signature rage-rap sound

  • 2022: Released debut studio album X, featuring hits like "Delinquent" and "New"

  • 2023: Released sophomore album A Great Chaos, debuting at #11 on the Billboard 200

  • 2024: Surpassed 10 million monthly listeners on Spotify, establishing himself as a leader in the rage rap genre

Musical Style & Impact


Ken Carson's music is characterized by distorted bass, synth-heavy production, and his distinctive vocal delivery which often employs auto-tune and melodic rapping. Tracks like "Yale", "Rock N Roll", and "Freestyle 2" showcase his ability to combine trap influences with punk-like energy. His style has helped define the emerging "rage" subgenre of hip-hop, influencing a new generation of artists and expanding on the sound pioneered by artists like Playboi Carti and Lil Uzi Vert.


Essential Listening

For Newcomers: "Yale," "South Beach," "Delinquent," "Freestyle 2"

For The Dive: "Murda Musik," "Paranoid," "New," "Till I Die"

Hidden Gems: "Pdbmh," "Butterfly," "Hella," "Going Schitz"


Legacy


Though still early in his career, Ken Carson has already established himself as a key figure in the evolution of trap music and rage rap. As a frontrunner of Playboi Carti's Opium collective alongside Destroy Lonely, Carson is helping reshape the sound of hip-hop for a new generation. His high-energy performances and distinctive visual aesthetic have created a cult-like following, particularly among Gen Z listeners. As he continues to evolve as an artist, Carson's influence on the underground-to-mainstream pipeline of hip-hop remains significant.
