JUICE WRLD

The Melodic Freestyle King


Jarad Anthony Higgins, known professionally as Juice WRLD, was a generational talent who blended hip-hop, trap, and emo to create a sound that captured the emotional complexity of his generation. His uncanny ability to freestyle for hours and craft melodic, emotionally vulnerable songs made him one of music's fastest-rising stars before his tragic death in 2019.


Rise to Fame


Juice WRLD began releasing music on SoundCloud in 2015 under the name JuicetheKidd before adopting his more well-known moniker. His breakthrough came in 2018 with the emotional single "Lucid Dreams," which peaked at #2 on the Billboard Hot 100 and became one of the most-streamed songs of the decade. The success of "Lucid Dreams" led to a bidding war among record labels, with Juice eventually signing a $3 million deal with Interscope Records. His debut album Goodbye & Good Riddance (2018) further cemented his place in hip-hop, followed by his second album Death Race for Love (2019), which debuted at #1 on the Billboard 200.


Career Milestones

  • 2017: Released breakout EP 9 9 9 featuring "Lucid Dreams" and "All Girls Are The Same"

  • 2018: Released debut album Goodbye & Good Riddance, achieving platinum status

  • 2018: Collaborated with Future on joint mixtape WRLD on Drugs, reaching #2 on Billboard 200

  • 2019: Released sophomore album Death Race for Love, debuting at #1 on Billboard 200

  • 2019: Passed away on December 8, at the age of 21

  • 2020: Posthumous album Legends Never Die released, achieving one of the biggest streaming weeks in history

  • 2021: Second posthumous album Fighting Demons released, featuring appearances from Justin Bieber and BTS

Musical Style & Impact


Juice WRLD's music was characterized by his melodic rapping, emotionally candid lyrics, and exceptional freestyle abilities. His sound drew from diverse influences including emo-rap, trap, and pop-punk, with lyrics that openly addressed mental health struggles, drug use, and heartbreak. His incredible ability to freestyle for hours—as demonstrated in his legendary hour-long freestyle on Tim Westwood TV—showcased his natural talent and quick thinking. Despite his short career, Juice WRLD left an indelible mark on hip-hop, helping to normalize emotional vulnerability and mental health discussions in a genre that often prized stoicism.

Legacy


Despite his tragically short career, Juice WRLD's impact on music has been profound. His willingness to be emotionally vulnerable helped reshape hip-hop's approach to mental health, while his melodic innovations have influenced countless artists. His music continues to resonate with millions, with songs like "Lucid Dreams" surpassing two billion streams on Spotify. With a vast catalog of unreleased material—reportedly over 2,000 songs—Juice WRLD's artistic vision continues to reach fans through carefully curated posthumous releases. Beyond the numbers, his legacy lives on through the Juice WRLD Fund, established by his mother to support young people struggling with mental health issues, addiction, and substance abuse.
