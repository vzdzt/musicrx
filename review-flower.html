FLOWER BOY

Tyler, the Creator - Flower Boy


On July 21, 2017, Tyler, The Creator released Flower Boy, a 14-track opus that peaked at #2 on the Billboard 200 and earned a Grammy nomination. Following the chaotic energy of his earlier work, this album marked a vibrant, introspective shift. Here’s a deep dive into this colorful, soulful masterpiece:


The Messaging and Theme


The album’s cover—Tyler in a field of sunflowers—sets the tone: a journey of growth and self-discovery. Flower Boy is Tyler shedding his shock-rap skin, embracing vulnerability and identity. Tracks like “See You Again” and “Garden Shed” explore unrequited love and queerness, while “Where This Flower Blooms” reflects on his rise. “Boredom” tackles loneliness with a tender touch. It’s a coming-of-age tale—Tyler as a flower boy, blooming amid chaos, balancing beauty with raw honesty.


Production and Sound


Entirely produced by Tyler, The Creator, with assists from Rex Orange County and others, Flower Boy is a lush, genre-blending soundscape. “Foreword” kicks off with gritty synths and strings, while “911 / Mr. Lonely” pairs funky bass with dreamy chords. “Who Dat Boy” thumps with dark energy, and “November” glows with jazzy warmth. Tyler’s voice—gruff yet melodic—dances over these beats, blending hip-hop, jazz, and neo-soul into a vivid, cinematic palette. It’s a leap from Cherry Bomb’s abrasiveness, embracing polish and nuance.


Features


Flower Boy is rich with guests who enhance Tyler’s vision. Frank Ocean glides on “Where This Flower Blooms” and “911 / Mr. Lonely,” while Kali Uchis adds sweetness to “See You Again.” A$AP Rocky brings swagger to “Who Dat Boy,” and Rex Orange County shines on “Boredom.” Lil Wayne, Estelle, and Anna of the North add flavor, but Tyler remains the heart—these voices bloom around his narrative.


Tracks to Watch

  • “See You Again” (feat. Kali Uchis) - A dreamy, lovelorn anthem with timeless charm.

  • “Who Dat Boy” (feat. A$AP Rocky) - A ferocious, beat-switching banger.

  • “Garden Shed” (feat. Estelle) - A tender, introspective gem of self-discovery.

  • “911 / Mr. Lonely” (feat. Frank Ocean) - A funky, heartfelt standout.

Previous Works


Tyler’s journey began with Bastard (2009), followed by Goblin (2011), Wolf (2013), and Cherry Bomb (2015), a run of abrasive, Odd Future-fueled chaos. Flower Boy softens Cherry Bomb’s rough edges, trading shock for soul and melody. Where past works leaned on provocation, this album blooms with maturity—Tyler evolving from a rebel to an artist unafraid to bare his soul.


Summary


Flower Boy is a 47-minute bouquet—14 tracks of vibrant production and raw emotion. It’s Tyler at his most refined, crafting a cohesive, introspective journey that balances beauty with bite. Every song shines, though “Pothole” feels slightly less essential. This isn’t just an album—it’s a transformation, showcasing Tyler’s growth as a producer and storyteller. It’s lush, honest, and captivating—a flower boy’s blooming triumph.


Ratings

Originality (19/20): A bold, genre-blending shift with Tyler’s unique stamp.

Production Quality (20/20): Flawless—lush, detailed, and perfectly crafted.

Lyrics and Messaging (19/20): Poignant and personal, with rare lighter moments.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): A rich, replayable experience with broad appeal.

Consistency (19/20): Near-perfect, with every track serving the vibe.

Overall Rating (96/100): 9.6/10

Flower Boy isn’t just Tyler’s best—it’s a career-defining pivot, a beautiful bloom from a once-prickly artist. It’s near-perfect, blending chaos and calm into a sound that’s uniquely his. In Tyler’s discography, this stands as a radiant high—a floral testament to growth and genius.
