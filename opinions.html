OPINIONS

Hot Takes

Streaming platforms like Spotify have turned music into background noise, not art—over 100 million songs uploaded by 2025, and most get less than 100 plays.


Drake's dominance is overrated; his 75 billion streams are inflated by playlist algorithms, not raw talent—Kendrick's catalog outclasses him pound-for-pound.


Taylor Swift's re-recordings are a genius cash grab, but they've diluted her legacy—fans are buying the same songs twice while she's laughing to the bank with $1 billion+.


Hip-hop's golden age ended in the 2010s; trap's repetition and mumble rap killed lyrical innovation—only 5% of Top 100 tracks in 2024 had bars worth quoting.


Auto-tune isn't a crutch, it's a revolution—Kanye and T-Pain proved it's an instrument, and purists crying about "real singing" are stuck in the 90s.
