A GREAT CHAOS

A Great Chaos: Ken Carson’s Ferocious Descent into Opium Madness


Unleashed October 13, 2023 via Opium and Interscope, Ken Carson’s A Great Chaos—an 18-track, 46-minute tempest—hit 205,000 first-week streams, peaking at No. 11 on the Billboard 200. Certified Platinum by March 16, 2025, its 2.9 billion streams and X hype cement its dark red reign. Fueled by F1LTHY and Opium’s finest, it’s Carson’s breakout, outshining his debut. Veazy’s 2025 take: a chaotic banger still tearing through the underground.


The Messaging and Theme


The gritty, low-res cover of Carson’s grill on A Great Chaos screams defiance—a plunge into hedonism and havoc. “Jennifer’s Body” snarls “I’m a dark knight,” a horror-tinged flex, while “Fighting My Demons” rages “I’m tryna win this fight.” “Paranoid” hints at unease—“They watchin’ me”—beneath the bravado of “Me N My Kup.” It’s not poetry; it’s a Gen Z war cry of excess and fleeting introspection, steeped in Opium’s haze. By 2025, X fans call it “Carson’s chaotic scripture,” its dark red pulse still electrifying mosh pits.


Production and Sound


Crafted by F1LTHY, Starboy, Outtatown, and more in Atlanta studios, A Great Chaos unleashes a rage rap assault. “Lose It” thunders with distorted bass, “Singapore” twists trap into a glitchy nightmare, and “Nightcore” races with hyperpop edge. “Jennifer’s Body” stutters like a broken machine, Carson’s Auto-Tuned mumble weaving into the chaos. It’s sharper than X’s dull sprawl, echoing Whole Lotta Red but rawer. The 18 tracks risk monotony, yet in 2025, its dark red sonic storm still fuels the Opium wave.


Features


A Great Chaos keeps it tight with Destroy Lonely and Lil Uzi Vert. Lonely’s icy “I’m on X” on “Singapore” and three other cuts complements Carson’s snarl, though it teeters on overkill. Uzi’s quirky “Yeah, let’s go” on “Like This” adds a jolt, subtle yet electric. These Opium allies amplify the dark red chaos without stealing Carson’s throne—he owns this mosh pit solo.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Jennifer’s Body”, “Fighting My Demons”— Jagged glitches and pounding bass hit instant, Carson’s dark red snarl dragging rookies into rage rap’s wild abyss with feral, catchy hooks.

For The Dive: “Lose It”, “Singapore”— Blown-out beats and eerie synths dig deep, Carson’s dark red chaos swirling with Opium flair for fans craving the album’s rawest cuts.

Hidden Gems: “Paranoid”, “Nightcore”— Subtle unease and hyperpop jolts shine, these dark red tracks unveiling Carson’s range amid the noise, gems for the attentive ear.

Latest Obsessions: “Jennifer’s Body (Live 2025)”— Lollapalooza 2025’s dark red strobes fuel Carson’s glitchy roar—a viral moment Veazy’s looping, its chaos still igniting crowds in real time.


Previous Works


Ken Carson debuted with Teen X (2020) EP, raw rage roots, then Project X (2021) with “Rock N Roll.” His first studio effort, X (2022), stumbled with flat beats like “The End,” peaking at No. 115. A Great Chaos refines that sound, tighter and fiercer, hitting No. 11. It sets up Overseas (2024), amplifying his dark red ascent in 2025.
