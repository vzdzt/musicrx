ETERNAL ATAKE

LIL UZI VERT - ETERNAL ATAKE


On March 6, 2020, Lil Uzi Vert finally dropped Eternal Atake, an 18-track odyssey that landed after years of delays, leaks, and fan frenzy. Following the success of Luv Is Rage 2, this album blasted Uzi into a sci-fi stratosphere, debuting at #1 on the Billboard 200. Here's a deep dive into this otherworldly masterpiece:


The Messaging and Theme


The album's cover—a cult-like logo with a UFO key—sets the stage for an intergalactic escape. Eternal Atake is Uzi embracing his alien persona, weaving tales of hedonism, heartbreak, and transcendence. Tracks like "Baby Pluto" and "Lo Mein" flex his extraterrestrial swagger, while "Homecoming" and "Celebration Station" hint at a longing for connection amid the chaos. It's not a linear story but a vibe-driven journey—Uzi as a space-bound rockstar, dodging earthly woes with diamonds and lean. This is his mythology, bold and unapologetic.


Production and Sound


Produced by a team including Chief Keef, Brandon Finessin, and Bugz Ronin, Eternal Atake is a sonic spaceship of trap and melodic rap. The beats are futuristic—"Baby Pluto" thumps with glitchy 808s, while "That Way" flips Backstreet Boys into a euphoric banger. "Silly Watch" buzzes with video game synths, and "Prices" drips with eerie menace. Uzi's voice—high-pitched, Auto-Tuned, and layered with ad-libs—dances over these soundscapes, blending aggression with melody. It's a polished evolution of Luv Is Rage 2's chaos, leaning into a cohesive, space-age vibe.


Features


Eternal Atake is a solo mission save for one guest: Syd of The Internet, who lends a sultry hook to "Urgency." Her smooth vocals contrast Uzi's frenetic energy, adding a rare moment of calm to the storm. The lack of features keeps the focus on Uzi, a bold choice that pays off—his charisma and versatility carry the album from start to finish. This is his galaxy, and he's the lone star shining.


Tracks to Watch

  • "Baby Pluto" - A high-energy opener that sets the album's cosmic tone.

  • "Lo Mein" - A chaotic banger with Uzi's wildest flows and glitchy beats.

  • "That Way" - A clever sample flip that blends nostalgia with Uzi's flair.

  • "P2" - A reflective sequel to "XO TOUR Llif3" that hits the heart.

Previous Works


Uzi's ascent began with Luv Is Rage (2015), followed by the breakout Luv Is Rage 2 (2017), which gave us "XO TOUR Llif3" and cemented his emo-rap stardom. Mixtapes like The Perfect LUV Tape (2016) honed his melodic trap style. Eternal Atake builds on this legacy, swapping Luv Is Rage 2's raw angst for a more polished, conceptual glow. Where past works thrived on spontaneity, this album aims higher—less a mixtape vibe, more a galactic statement.


Summary


Eternal Atake is a 54-minute warp-speed trip—18 tracks of interstellar flexing and emotional flickers. It's Uzi at his peak, delivering the payoff fans waited years for with a sound that's both familiar and futuristic. The album's length and mid-tier tracks—like "Bust Me"—can drag, but its highs are stratospheric. Eternal Atake doesn't rewrite Uzi's playbook; it refines it, proving he's a genre-bending force who can take us to the stars. This is his victory lap, beamed from another planet.


Ratings

Originality (18/20): A fresh sci-fi twist on Uzi's sound, though rooted in his past.

Production Quality (19/20): Stellar and cohesive, with slight dips in variety.

Lyrics and Messaging (17/20): Catchy and emotive, but depth takes a backseat to vibe.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): A thrilling, replayable blast from takeoff to landing.

Consistency (17/20): Strong peaks, but some filler stalls the momentum.

Overall Rating (90/100): 9.0/10

Eternal Atake isn't flawless—it's too long, too vibe-heavy at times—but it's a dazzling showcase of Lil Uzi Vert's star power. It's a love letter to his fans, a middle finger to the delays, and a bold leap into his alien lore. In a career of highs, this stands as a cosmic peak—a testament to Uzi's ability to turn hype into something eternal.
