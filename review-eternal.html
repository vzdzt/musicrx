Introduction

Dropped March 6, 2020, via Generation Now and Atlantic, Lil Uzi Vert’s Eternal Atake—an 18-track, 54-minute trap odyssey—hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 288,000 first-week units. Powered by Chief Keef and Brandon Finessin, it’s 2x Platinum by March 2025 with 3.2 billion streams. Rolling Loud 2025 keeps it trending on X (@36966_).

Production

Chief Keef, Brandon Finessin, and Bugz Ronin craft a trap spaceship in Quality Control Studios and Uzi’s home studio. “Baby Pluto” thumps with glitchy 808s, “That Way” flips Backstreet Boys, and “Silly Watch” pulses with video game synths. Finessin’s futuristic beats shine, though variety dips slightly.

Messaging and Theme

The UFO key logo cover sets a cosmic tone. Eternal Atake blends hedonism and heartbreak: “Baby Pluto” boasts alien swagger, “Lo Mein” flexes chaos, and “Homecoming” reveals vulnerability. X fans (@36966_) in 2025 call it “Uzi’s starship manifesto.”

Features

Eternal Atake is near-solo, with only Syd’s sultry hook on “Urgency” contrasting Uzi’s frenetic energy. His versatile flow—chaotic in “Lo Mein,” reflective in “P2”—keeps the focus on his purple-hued charisma, amplifying his cosmic vision.

Track Highlights

For Newcomers

“Baby Pluto”, “That Way”—Glitchy 808s and a euphoric Backstreet Boys sample hook new listeners with Uzi’s playful, cosmic energy.

For Deep Cuts

“Lo Mein”, “Silly Watch”—Chaotic beats and video game synths ignite Uzi’s frenetic flows for fans craving his high-octane trap.

Hidden Gems

“Homecoming”, “Celebration Station”—Melodic undertones and yearning vocals reveal Uzi’s emotional depth beneath the galactic flex.

2025 Obsession

“That Way (Live 2025)”—Rolling Loud 2025’s viral set (X @36966_) electrifies with Uzi’s soaring, euphoric performance.

Previous Works

Uzi’s Luv Is Rage (2015) and Luv Is Rage 2 (2017) built his emo-rap buzz with “XO TOUR Llif3.” Eternal Atake elevated his melodic trap, paving the way for Pink Tape (2023).

Legacy & Reception

Pitchfork’s 7.2/10 called it “Uzi’s cosmic leap,” Rolling Stone praised its sheen. X fans (@36966_) in 2025 hail “a purple classic,” with 2x Platinum status and a viral Rolling Loud 2025 “That Way” set.

Awards Recognition

Eternal Atake earned 2021 Grammy nominations for Best Rap Album and Best Rap Song (“That Way”), per Grammy records.
