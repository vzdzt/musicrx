MUSIC: Playboi Carti’s Chaotic Triumph Unleashed


Released March 14, 2025 via AWGE and Interscope, Playboi Carti’s MUSIC—a 30-track, 78-minute rap odyssey—is projected to sell 302,000 units in its first week (excluding bundles), per HITS Daily Double, eyeing a No. 1 Billboard 200 debut. By March 21, 2025, it’s racked up 228.7 million Spotify streams, the year’s biggest single-day haul (139.3 million). Carti’s red-hot reign peaks here, debuted live at Rolling Loud LA. Veazy’s take: this chaotic titan owns 2025’s soundscape, its raw intensity a seismic shift from past works.


The Messaging and Theme


MUSIC is Carti’s red-hot declaration of sonic supremacy and defiance. “EVIL J0RDAN” taunts “I’m so high, gotta hide my face,” reveling in reckless abandon, while “RATHER LIE” with The Weeknd muses “Lost my soul in the night,” blending existential haze with bravado. “CHARGE DEM HOES A FEE” boasts “I’m charging fees for the vibe,” doubling down on his untouchable persona. Themes of rebellion, identity, and chaos roar through its 30 tracks. In 2025, fans on X call it “the soundtrack to anarchy,” with Rolling Loud LA clips and TikTok trends amplifying its cultural quake—a raw, unfiltered Carti unleashed.


Production and Sound


F1LTHY, Cardo, and Bnyx shape MUSIC’s 30 tracks, primarily recorded in a cave-like studio in Paris, with Swamp Izzo’s ad-libs punctuating the chaos. Rumors circulating online suggest that Carti used AI on “FINE SH*T” and “RATHER LIE,” leaving fans with mixed reactions, many in shock. Trap’s booming 808s meet punk’s jagged edges in “HBA,” “CRUSH,” and “BACKR00MS” (by Bnyx and F1LTHY). Refining Whole Lotta Red, it’s a red-hot assault that echoes in 2025’s underground via Yeat and Ken Carson.


Features


MUSIC’s 30 tracks boast heavy hitters amplifying Carti’s red-hot vision. The Weeknd croons “Tell me lies, I’ll believe” on “RATHER LIE,” his ethereal tone cutting through distortion. Travis Scott growls “Backrooms, we don’t play” on “BACKR00MS,” syncing with Carti’s frenetic flow. Kendrick Lamar taunts “I’m a trickster, watch me move” on “MOJO JOJO,” adding Compton edge. Young Thug slimes through “GOOD CREDIT,” while Carti keeps them as fuel for his chaos, not spotlights, weaving their voices into his warped tapestry.

Previous Works


Carti’s road to MUSIC started with 2017’s Playboi Carti (No. 12, 28,000 units), led by “Magnolia.” Die Lit (2018) hit No. 3 with 61,000 units, its trap minimalism shining via “Shoota.” Whole Lotta Red (2020) took No. 1, 100,000 units, with punk-rap chaos like “Sky.” MUSIC (2025), projected at 302,000 units, refines that edge into a red-hot behemoth, dwarfing past debuts. Its 30-track sprawl shapes 2025’s underground, influencing acts like Destroy Lonely.

Ratings

Originality (18/20): Carti’s 30-track punk-trap epic pushes boundaries with a chaotic sonic palette, evolving Whole Lotta Red’s blueprint into a sprawling, genre-blending odyssey—though it doesn’t fully break new ground, its ambition is undeniable.

Production Quality (19/20): F1LTHY, Cardo, and Bnyx deliver a pristine mix of booming 808s, glitchy synths, and jagged punk edges, especially on “BACKR00MS” and “CRUSH.” The Paris cave-like studio setting adds a raw, experimental sheen, with minor mixing hiccups barely noticeable amidst the sonic intensity.

Lyrics and Messaging (16/20): “EVIL J0RDAN” roars with attitude, but the album’s focus on vibe over depth means lyrical substance takes a backseat. While tracks like “RATHER LIE” hint at emotional complexity, the lack of Die Lit’s quirky flair holds it back slightly.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): “BACKR00MS” slams with replayable energy, its TikTok virality a testament to its infectious chaos. From “CRUSH”’s hooks to “OPM BABI”’s live intensity, the highs are electrifying—though the lengthy runtime can drag if not in the right mood.

Consistency (17/20): Standouts like “HBA” and “CRUSH” maintain a fiery thread, but 30 tracks breed filler, with some cuts feeling redundant. Still, the album’s cohesive chaotic vision ensures the dips don’t derail its overall impact.

Overall Rating (89/100): 8.9/10

MUSIC earns its 8.9/10 through its fearless ambition and cultural resonance, delivering a punk-trap odyssey that electrifies with tracks like “EVIL J0RDAN” and “BACKR00MS.” The production’s raw intensity, paired with impactful features from The Weeknd and Kendrick Lamar, creates a chaotic masterpiece that dominates 2025’s soundscape. Despite some lyrical shallowness and occasional filler, its replay value and influence on the underground make it a triumph worth celebrating.
