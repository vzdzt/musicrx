CALL ME IF YOU GET LOST

Call Me If You Get Lost: Tyler’s Sunlit Voyage of Swagger and Soul


Dropped on June 25, 2021 via Columbia and A Boy Is a Gun, Tyler, The Creator’s sixth album Call Me If You Get Lost is a 16-track, 52-minute hip-hop odyssey, powered by Tyler, The Creator and DJ Drama. Post-IGOR’s Grammy win, it hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 169,000 first-week units. By March 15, 2025, its 2x Platinum status, 2.8 billion streams, and X buzz (@36966_) keep its pink fire blazing. Veazy here—this album’s a bold gem that reshaped Tyler, still pulsing with swagger.


The Messaging and Theme


The cover—a faux “Tyler Baudelaire” ID—hints at poetry, mirroring Call Me If You Get Lost’s lavish hip-hop manifesto. It’s a brash flex with a tender edge, roaring in “Corso”’s “I’m rich as fuck” and softening with “Sweet”’s “You’re my sweetheart.” “Wilshire” and “Lemonhead” confess—“My heart’s on the line”—blending swagger with soul. It’s a genre-bending storm, a pink testament to Tyler’s prime that still sparks on X (@36966_) in 2025 as “Tyler’s passport.”


Production and Sound


Tyler, The Creator, DJ Drama, and Jay Versace forge Call Me If You Get Lost’s sound—jazz, soul, and hip-hop chaos across its 16 tracks. Recorded at Beverly Hills Studios in LA and tour buses on the road, “Lumberjack” bangs with distorted bass, “Wusyaname” glides on silky synths, and “Sweet” soars with horns—Tyler’s fluid flow slicing through like a breeze. Tyler’s lush beats became a rap staple by 2025, though guests crowd the mix slightly, a dent in its brilliance.


Features


Call Me If You Get Lost shines with Lil Wayne, 42 Dugg, and YoungBoy Never Broke Again, each adding fire to Tyler’s pink inferno. Lil Wayne lifts “Hot Wind Blows” with grit—“Tunechi make it easy”—while 42 Dugg fuels “Lemonhead” with sharp bars. YoungBoy adds melody to “Wusyaname”. Tyler’s delivery—witty, smooth, commanding—dominates, ensuring features enhance rather than overshadow his vision.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Wusyaname”, “Lumberjack”— Silky R&B hooks paired with YoungBoy’s croon, distorted beats roar with intensity—Tyler’s playful charisma grabs new listeners, pulling them into his vibrant, genre-blending hip-hop world effortlessly.

For The Dive: “Corso”, “Juggernaut”— Jazzy horns blast with cinematic flair, Uzi’s chaotic energy erupts alongside Tyler’s bold swagger, offering fans a deeper dive into his confident, genre-bending soundscape with electrifying vibes.

Hidden Gems: “Sweet / I Thought”, “Wilshire”— Horns weave a romantic tapestry, raw confessions sting with vulnerability—Tyler’s soulful side glows brightly, offering underrated depth beneath the album’s brash surface for introspective listeners to savor.

Latest Obsessions: “Lumberjack (Live 2025)”— Coachella 2025’s viral set explodes with “Lumberjack”—Tyler’s pink energy electrifies the crowd, his commanding stage presence shining, leaving Veazy replaying this bold, unforgettable performance over and over.


Previous Works


Tyler’s road to Call Me If You Get Lost ran through Flower Boy (2017) and IGOR (2019) with Tyler. Hits like “See You Again” and “Earfquake” built his soulful buzz, while CMIYGL redefined his sound, paving the way for Chromakopia (2024).

Ratings

Originality (19/20): Mixtape grit fuses with soulful jazz—“Corso” twists swagger into something fresh—a bold leap from Tyler’s past, pushing hip-hop boundaries with innovative flair and confidence.

Production Quality (20/20): Jazz horns and silky beats dazzle—“Sweet” flows with lush elegance—Tyler’s production genius crafts a flawless sonic masterpiece, blending genres seamlessly for an immersive listening experience.

Lyrics and Messaging (18/20): Witty flexes in “Corso” hit hard, “Wilshire” bares Tyler’s soul with depth, but brash bravado occasionally overshadows the album’s potential for even deeper lyrical and emotional insight.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): Infectious vibes hook instantly—“Wusyaname” lingers with charm—a joyous ride for all, though guest-heavy tracks slightly crowd Tyler’s shine, dipping the album’s solo replay value just a bit.

Consistency (18/20): Flow stays strong from “Lumberjack”’s intensity to “Sweet”’s romance, delivering a cohesive vibe, though late tracks dip slightly in momentum, breaking the album’s otherwise steady, electrifying pace.

Overall Rating (94/100): 9.4/10

Call Me If You Get Lost is a hip-hop triumph—Tyler’s bold soul ignites a pink blaze pulsing with flair. Picture a 2025 Coachella stage, his smooth voice commanding a vibrant crowd—lush, audacious, replayable. Veazy’s blasting this in 2025, chasing its pink glow.
