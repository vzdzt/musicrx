Introduction

Unleashed June 10, 2008 via Young Money, Cash Money, and Universal Motown, Lil Wayne’s Tha Carter III—a 16-track, 77-minute titan—sold 1.01 million first-week copies, hitting No. 1 on the Billboard 200. Certified 8x Platinum by April 2025 (RIAA), its 14.2 billion streams and 2025 tour hype cement its reign, per X posts (@36966_).

Production

Kanye West, Bangladesh, Swizz Beatz, The Alchemist, and more at The Hit Factory forge Tha Carter III—Southern grit meets pop gloss. “A Milli” loops hypnotic, “Lollipop” gleams Auto-Tuned, “Got Money” bounces bold, and “Dr. Carter” hums eerie. Wayne’s voice—gruff, elastic—sharpens Tha Carter II’s edge, ruling 2025’s rap echo.

Messaging and Theme

The baby Wayne cover—tattooed, crowned—sets Tha Carter III’s tone: a coronation of rap’s wild king. “A Milli” boasts “I’m a venereal disease,” pure bravado, “Mr. Carter” claims “I’m right here with my crown on,” “Tie My Hands” mourns post-Katrina, and “Mrs. Officer” charms “Wee-ooh-wee.” X (@36966_): “Wayne’s crown shines.”

Features

Tha Carter III flexes with Jay-Z on “Mr. Carter”—“Good morning”—T-Pain on “Got Money”—“I got money”—Babyface on “Comfortable,” Robin Thicke on “Tie My Hands,” plus Bobby Valentino, Busta Rhymes, and Fabolous. They amplify Wayne’s royal blaze.

Previous Works

Tha Block Is Hot (1999) sparked with “Drop It Like It’s Hot,” Lights Out (2000) grew with “Get Off the Corner,” 500 Degreez (2002) flexed “Way of Life,” Tha Carter (2004) rose with “Go DJ,” and Tha Carter II (2005, No. 2 Billboard 200) peaked with “Fireman” (No. 32 Hot 100). Tha Carter III crowns it No. 1, per Billboard.

Legacy & Reception

Metacritic’s 84/100 and Rolling Stone’s 4/5 hailed Tha Carter III “a rap classic,” X (@36966_) in 2008 called it “Wayne’s throne.” By April 2025, with 8x Platinum status (RIAA), 14.2 billion streams, and a Rolling Loud 2025 “A Milli” revival, it’s a Southern giant, per Rolling Stone.

Awards Recognition

Tha Carter III won Best Rap Album and Best Rap Song (“Lollipop”) in 2009, nabbed Album of the Year nomination. Its 8x Platinum status and 14.2 billion streams by 2025 affirm Wayne’s reign, per Grammy records.
