2014 Forest Hills Drive

4.8B Streams 3x Platinum 353K First Week #1 Billboard 200

Introduction

J. Cole’s 2014 Forest Hills Drive hit via Roc Nation, Columbia, and Dreamville. This 13-track, 64-minute memoir moved 353,000 units, topping the Billboard 200. As of March 29, 2025, it’s 3x Platinum with 4.8 billion streams. A soulful classic cooked with Vinylz, still blazing in 2025.

Production & Sound

Cole, Vinylz, Illmind, and Ron Gilmore crafted this at Perfect Sound and Jungle City Studios. “No Role Modelz” bangs, “Apparently” hums, and “Fire Squad” hits hard—smooth yet raw, a 2025 introspective blueprint.

Track Highlights

Newcomers: “No Role Modelz,” “Love Yourz”—jazzy hooks pull you in.

Deep Dive: “Wet Dreamz,” “03’ Adolescence”—raw soul hits hard.

Gems: “Fire Squad,” “Apparently”—punchy depth shines.

2025 Obsession: “Love Yourz (Live)”—Fayetteville tour fire.
