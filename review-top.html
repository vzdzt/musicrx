TOP

Top: YoungBoy’s Stormy Ascent to Trap Royalty


Dropped on September 11, 2020 via Never Broke Again and Atlantic, NBA YoungBoy’s third album Top is a 21-track, 63-minute trap juggernaut, powered by TayTayMadeIt and Dmac. Post-AI YoungBoy 2’s chart-topping grit, it hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 126,000 first-week units. By March 15, 2025, its Platinum status, 2.5 billion streams, and X hype (@36966_) keep its gray fire blazing. Veazy here—this album’s a raw peak that reshaped YoungBoy, still pulsing with street energy.


The Messaging and Theme


The cover—YoungBoy under a stormy sky—sets a gritty tone, mirroring Top’s raw trap manifesto. It’s a defiant flex with a pained edge, roaring in “House Arrest Tingz”’s “I’m still thuggin’” and slashing with “Dead Trollz”’s venom. “Kacey Talk” and “All In” shift gears—“I got all these bitches”—blending charisma with scars—“You could never know.” It’s a chaotic street diary, unfiltered and fierce, a gray testament to YoungBoy’s resilience that still sparks on X (@36966_) in 2025 as “trap’s stormy crown.”


Production and Sound


TayTayMadeIt, Dmac, and Mike Laury forge Top’s sound—Southern trap chaos across its 21 tracks. Recorded at Baton Rouge Studios in Louisiana and home setups under legal strain, “Dead Trollz” thumps with distorted 808s, “My Window” haunts with eerie keys, and “Kacey Talk” glides on soulful samples—YoungBoy’s raspy flow slicing through like a blade. TayTayMadeIt’s hard-hitting beats became a trap staple by 2025, though the 63-minute runtime drags slightly, a dent in its raw power.


Features


Top leans light on features with Snoop Dogg, Lil Wayne, each adding flair to YoungBoy’s gray inferno. Snoop Dogg lifts “Callin” with laid-back cool—“What’s good, homie?”—while Lil Wayne fuels “My Window” with sharp bars—“I’m a lion!” YoungBoy’s delivery—raspy, urgent, commanding—dominates, ensuring features enhance rather than overshadow his vision.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Kacey Talk”, “Dead Trollz”— Soulful hooks blend with booming 808s, YoungBoy’s fierce energy and raw charisma shine, instantly hooking new listeners into his gritty trap world with unfiltered street vibes.

For The Dive: “My Window”, “House Arrest Tingz”— Lil Wayne’s sharp verse cuts through haunting keys, YoungBoy’s defiant grit roars loud, offering fans a deep dive into his stormy, unapologetic soul with raw emotion.

Hidden Gems: “All In”, “The Last Backyard…”— Melodic pain pierces through soft beats, guitars lift YoungBoy’s raw defiance, these underrated tracks reveal his scarred heart, resonating quietly with listeners seeking deeper emotional layers.

Latest Obsessions: “Dead Trollz (Live 2025)”— At Baton Rouge 2025, YoungBoy snarls “Troll music!”—his gray energy ignites the crowd, a viral moment of raw power that keeps Veazy replaying this fiery performance.


Previous Works


NBA YoungBoy’s road to Top ran through 38 Baby (2016) and AI YoungBoy (2017) with TayTayMadeIt. Hits like “Murder” and “No Smoke” built his street buzz, while Top redefined his sound, paving the way for Sincerely, Kentrell (2021).

Legacy & Reception


Critics split—Pitchfork’s 7.2/10 called it “YoungBoy’s grit refined,” Rolling Stone praised its rawness. X fans (@36966_) in 2020 dubbed it “trap’s gray storm,” a vibe alive in March 15, 2025, with Platinum, 2.5 billion streams, and a 2025 Baton Rouge set. A viral “Dead Trollz” show—YoungBoy snarling live—cements its street reign.

Ratings

Originality (16/20): Trap gets a polish with “Kacey Talk”’s soulful twist, but YoungBoy refines his roots rather than breaking new ground, sticking to his fierce, familiar street sound.

Production Quality (18/20): Beats hit hard—“Dead Trollz” thumps with raw 808s—TayTayMadeIt’s tight production shines, though some repetition in the lengthy tracklist slightly dulls the album’s overall edge.

Lyrics and Messaging (17/20): Raw scars in “All In” cut deep, street tales grip listeners, but YoungBoy’s bravado sometimes overshadows deeper insight, limiting the emotional range of his storytelling.

Enjoyment Factor (18/20): High energy hooks hard—“My Window” bites with intensity—a thrilling trap ride for fans, though the sprawl of 21 tracks tests casual listeners’ attention at times.

Consistency (15/20): Bangers like “House Arrest Tingz” deliver raw fire, but filler tracks drag late in the album, causing momentum to dip despite YoungBoy’s consistently fierce and gritty flow.

Overall Rating (84/100): 8.4/10

Top is a trap titan—YoungBoy’s raw reign ignites a gray blaze pulsing with grit. Picture a 2025 Baton Rouge stage, his raspy snarl commanding a roaring crowd—fierce, flawed, replayable. Veazy’s blasting this in 2025, chasing its gray storm.
