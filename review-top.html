TOP

Top: NBA YoungBoy’s Stormy Ascent to Trap Royalty


On September 11, 2020, NBA YoungBoy unleashed Top, a 21-track juggernaut dropped amid legal battles and fervent fan demand. Following a torrent of prolific releases, this album cemented his reign in the trap scene with raw energy and emotional depth. As of March 16, 2025, let’s dive into this relentless Southern triumph.


The Messaging and Theme

The album’s cover—YoungBoy brooding against a stormy sky—evokes defiance, pain, and unyielding hustle. Top is NBA YoungBoy laying bare his soul, from street anthems like “House Arrest Tingz” to vulnerable confessions in “All In.” “Kacey Talk” radiates charisma, while “Dead Trollz” unleashes fury on his enemies. It’s less a story than a raw journal—YoungBoy’s life of loyalty, betrayal, and survival in Baton Rouge, delivered with a defiant snarl. This is his crowning moment, scarred yet unbowed.


Production and Sound

Crafted by producers like TayTayMadeIt, Dmac, and Mike Laury, Top delivers a sonic barrage rooted in Southern trap. The beats strike hard—“Dead Trollz” pulses with distorted 808s, while “My Window” weaves eerie keys over relentless drums. “Kacey Talk” transforms a soulful sample into a smooth flex, and “The Last Backyard…” rides a hypnotic guitar riff. YoungBoy’s voice—raspy, urgent, and melodic—cuts through the storm, shifting from fierce shouts to Auto-Tuned croons. It’s a polished evolution of his sound, balancing grit with broader appeal.


Features

Top keeps features minimal, spotlighting YoungBoy’s dominance. Snoop Dogg adds West Coast flair to “Callin,” while Lil Wayne brings veteran edge to “My Window.” These guests enhance without eclipsing, offering brief contrasts to YoungBoy’s intensity. The sparse collaborations underscore his self-reliance—Top is his domain, ruled with solitary force.


Tracks to Watch

  • “Kacey Talk” - Soulful sample ignites this charismatic flex, YoungBoy’s smooth flow radiating street confidence.

  • “Dead Trollz” - Distorted 808s fuel this ferocious diss, YoungBoy’s venom cutting deep with unrelenting fury.

  • “All In” - Melodic tones carry this poignant cry, baring YoungBoy’s pain with haunting resilience.

  • “My Window” (feat. Lil Wayne) - Eerie keys frame this haunting gem, Wayne’s verse sharpening YoungBoy’s emotional edge.

Previous Works

NBA YoungBoy’s ascent began with mixtapes like 38 Baby (2016), showcasing his gritty, melodic trap roots. Albums like AI YoungBoy (2017) and Until Death Call My Name (2018) grew his cult following with relentless output. Top refines this blueprint, swapping some lo-fi chaos for crisper production and wider appeal. Where earlier works were raw and unstructured, Top polishes his edge—a testament to YoungBoy’s growth while staying rooted in his Baton Rouge origins.


Summary

Top delivers a fierce 63-minute onslaught—21 tracks of unfiltered emotion and trap dominance. NBA YoungBoy reigns at his peak, turning pain into anthems and proving his resilience amid turmoil. The length can falter, with some tracks blending into the haze, but peaks like “Kacey Talk” hit with force. As of March 16, 2025, Top doesn’t reinvent his sound—it accelerates it, cementing YoungBoy as a voice for the streets, scarred yet soaring.


Ratings

Originality (16/20): Trap sound evolves with polished refinement, building on YoungBoy’s roots while exploring familiar terrain.

Production Quality (18/20): Hard-hitting beats pulse with precision, marred only by occasional repetition.

Lyrics and Messaging (17/20): Raw honesty and street tales shine, though depth fluctuates across the tracklist.

Enjoyment Factor (18/20): Gripping energy hooks fans, tempered by its runtime for casual listeners.

Consistency (15/20): Front-loaded strength wanes in the back half, with filler diluting its impact.

Overall Rating (84/100): 8.4/10

Top isn’t a revolutionary leap—it’s a testament to NBA YoungBoy’s relentless drive and magnetic presence. Flawed, sprawling, and unapologetic, it mirrors the man himself. In a crowded trap world, this stands as a raw, roaring declaration of YoungBoy’s enduring reign.
