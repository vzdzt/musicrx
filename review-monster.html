MONSTER

Monster: Future’s Lean-Soaked Descent Unleashed


Dropped October 28, 2014 via Freebandz, Future’s Monster—a 16-track, 58-minute mixtape—ignited trap’s dark soul. Post-Honest, it hit 2 million downloads fast, now boasting 3.2 billion streams and 2x Platinum status by March 16, 2025. With Metro Boomin at the helm, it’s Future’s raw rebirth. Veazy’s 2025 take: a red-hot chaos still burning.


The Messaging and Theme


A blood-red silhouette on Monster’s cover screams Future’s plunge into madness post-Ciara split. It’s rage and despair in lean-drenched hues—“Throw Away” flips “I did it my way” to heartbreak, “Codeine Crazy” slurs “I’m an addict and I can’t quit.” Ditching Honest’s pop gloss, it’s a visceral howl of defiance and excess, a trap king’s diary. X (@36966_) in 2025: “Future’s pain still bleeds red.”


Production and Sound


Metro Boomin leads Monster’s charge, joined by Southside, TM88, DJ Spinz, and Nard & B, crafting it at Tree Sound Studios. “Monster” snarls with feral 808s, “Codeine Crazy” drips melancholic synths over a Lana Del Rey sample, and “My Savages” thumps raw. Future’s Auto-Tuned wails fuse with dark trap beats, a red-hot shift from Honest’s sheen, shaping 2025’s gritty soundscape.


Features


Monster stays lean on guests, spotlighting Future’s red-hot fury. Lil Wayne slides in on “After That”—“Pussy wet, her nigga damp”—a slick foil to Future’s chaos. Solo-driven, it’s a bold flex, echoing Drake’s later minimalism, letting Future’s lean-soaked vision reign raw and unfiltered.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Monster”, “Codeine Crazy”— Booming 808s and lean-laced hooks grab quick, Future’s red-hot snarl reeling rookies into his trap abyss with force.

For The Dive: “Throw Away”, “My Savages”— Beat switches and raw loyalty cut deep, Future’s red-hot pain baring soul for fans craving depth.

Hidden Gems: “Radical”, “Hard”— Gritty energy and overlooked growls simmer, Future’s red-hot edge pulsing in cuts beneath the anthems.

Latest Obsessions: “Codeine Crazy (Live 2025)”— Future’s red-hot Rolling Loud 2025 wail shakes the crowd—a viral lean dream Veazy’s spinning now.


Previous Works


Dirty Sprite (2011) sparked with “Racks,” Astronaut Status (2012) flexed “Same Damn Time,” and Honest (2014) chased pop with “Move That Dope.” Monster reboots raw—red-hot trap fueling DS2 (2015).

Legacy & Reception


Pitchfork’s 8.2/10 praised Monster as “trap’s dark heart,” X (@36966_) in 2014 roared “Future’s lean Bible.” By March 16, 2025, with 2x Platinum, 3.2 billion streams, and a Rolling Loud 2025 “Codeine Crazy” moment, it’s a titan. Rawer than Honest, it birthed Young Thug’s chaos and 2025’s trap grit.

Ratings

Originality (17/20): “Codeine Crazy” twists trap raw, Future’s red-hot snarl breaks molds—builds on past, but its lean edge feels fresh.

Production Quality (19/20): “Monster” thumps, “Throw Away” switches—Metro’s red-hot beats blaze near perfect, with minor late dips like “Gangland.”

Lyrics and Messaging (16/20): “My Savages” snarls loyalty, red-hot pain cuts—repetition in “Hard” dulls the lyrical bite a bit.

Enjoyment Factor (18/20): “After That” hooks, “Radical” rumbles—red-hot chaos thrills slow and deep, though length tests some spins.

Consistency (17/20): “Monster” to “Codeine” flows tight, red-hot grit holds—tail-end tracks like “Wesley Presley” waver slightly.

Overall Rating (87/100): 8.7/10

Monster is Future’s red-hot rebirth—lean-soaked, raw, chaotic. Rolling Loud 2025’s “Codeine Crazy” roars anew, a 2025 beast unleashed. Veazy’s hooked—its dark trap soul isn’t tightest, but its realness redefined the game, echoing in 2025’s haze.
