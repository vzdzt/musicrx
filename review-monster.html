MONSTER

Monster: Future’s Lean-Soaked Descent into Trap Chaos


On October 28, 2014, Future dropped Monster, his ninth mixtape, under Freebandz Entertainment, igniting a legendary run that redefined trap music. Coming off the lukewarm reception to his 2014 album Honest, this 16-track project—executive produced by Metro Boomin—marked a gritty pivot, born from heartbreak and hedonism after his split with Ciara. As of March 10, 2025, Monster stands as a cornerstone of Future’s discography and a trap classic—let’s dive into its raw, intoxicating abyss.


The Messaging and Theme


The cover art—a blood-red silhouette of Future against a black void—sets the tone: this is a descent into darkness. Monster trades the pop-leaning aspirations of Honest for a visceral, drug-fueled diary of rage, sorrow, and defiance. Tracks like “Throw Away” and “Codeine Crazy” channel personal turmoil—Future’s breakup and struggles with addiction—into a monstrous persona that’s both villain and victim. It’s less about storytelling and more about raw emotion, painting a trap odyssey where excess and despair collide, cementing Future as the genre’s tortured king.


Production and Sound


Helmed by Metro Boomin, with beats from Southside, TM88, DJ Spinz, and Nard & B, Monster is a masterclass in dark, atmospheric trap. The production is thick with booming 808s, eerie synths, and haunting melodies—“Monster” snarls with menacing energy, while “Codeine Crazy” drips with melancholic lean-soaked vibes, sampling Lana Del Rey’s “Summertime Sadness.” Future’s auto-tuned croons and snarls blend into the beats, creating a hypnotic, otherworldly soundscape that’s as menacing as it is addictive, laying the blueprint for trap’s evolution.


Features


Monster is light on guests, letting Future carry the weight. The sole feature, Lil Wayne, drops a slick verse on “After That”, adding a touch of YMCMB flair with lines like “I’ma get that pussy wet, her other nigga just dampen that.” It’s not Wayne’s finest, but it complements Future’s relentless energy without stealing the spotlight. This minimal approach keeps the focus on Future’s unfiltered vision, proving he doesn’t need crutches to dominate—a rare feat in a genre often reliant on star power.


Tracks to Watch

  • “Monster” - Ferocious trap energy roars through this opener, its 808s pounding like a beast unleashed.

  • “Throw Away” - Heartbreak bleeds into this two-part titan, a beat switch flipping bravado to anguish.

  • “Codeine Crazy” - Lean-soaked sorrow crowns this standout, its haunting sample drowning in purple haze.

  • “My Savages” - Gritty loyalty pulses here, thunderous drums saluting Future’s ride-or-die crew.

Previous Works


Before Monster, Future built his name with mixtapes like Dirty Sprite (2011) and Astronaut Status (2012), showcasing his melodic trap flair, and his 2013 album Honest aimed for mainstream appeal with mixed results. Monster marks a hard reset—ditching pop polish for the street grit of his early days, amplified by Metro Boomin’s dark genius. It kicked off a trilogy with Beast Mode and 56 Nights, cementing Future’s shift from radio hopeful to trap icon, a pivot that would peak with 2015’s DS2.


Summary


Monster is a 56-minute plunge into Future’s psyche—unpolished, unapologetic, and unrelenting. It’s a mixtape that thrives on its rawness, blending street anthems with moments of startling vulnerability. While some tracks like “2 Pac” lean on familiar tropes, the highs—especially “Codeine Crazy” and “Throw Away”—are genre-defining. As of March 10, 2025, Monster remains a fan-favorite milestone, the spark of Future’s imperial phase, proving he’s at his best when he’s broken, bold, and unbothered by convention.


Ratings

Production Quality (19/20): Metro’s grim genius anchors this tape, its beats dripping with menace.

Lyrics and Messaging (16/20): Pain seeps through every croon, though repetition dulls the blade.

Enjoyment Factor (18/20): Relentless vibes hook you deep, its raw chaos a slow-burn thrill.

Consistency (17/20): Solid grit holds it together, with slight stumbles in the tail end.

Overall Rating (87/100): 8.7/10

Monster is Future clawing his way back—a lean-drenched, trap-fueled howl of defiance and despair. It’s not his tightest, but it’s his realest, a cornerstone that paved the way for greatness. Blast it loud, let the 808s rumble, and sink into the chaos of a king reborn.
