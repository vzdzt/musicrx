UP 2 MË

Up 2 Më: Yeat's Rage-Fueled Breakthrough


On September 10, 2021, Portland rapper Yeat dropped Up 2 Më, a 22-track debut album that stormed the underground with viral fury. Following a string of buzzworthy singles, this project thrust Yeat into the spotlight as a torchbearer of the rage rap wave. Here’s a deep dive into this chaotic, adrenaline-pumping triumph:


The Messaging and Theme


The album’s cover—a minimalist red and black swirl—hints at its vibe: a whirlwind of excess and emotion. Up 2 Më is Yeat reveling in his ascent, from flexing wealth in “Monëy So Big” to taunting foes in “Gët Busy.” Yet beneath the bravado, tracks like “Lët Ya Know” reveal flickers of vulnerability—“This could be my last song, ho”—amid tales of drugs and despair. It’s not deep poetry; it’s a raw snapshot of a 21-year-old riding fame’s high, fueled by lean and Tonkas. This is rage rap distilled—unapologetic, reckless, and defiantly youthful.


Production and Sound


With over 20 producers like BNYX, F1LTHY, and Synthetic, Up 2 Më is a sonic explosion of rage beats—think distorted 808s, glitchy synths, and omnipresent bells. “Turban” screeches with warped energy, while “Swërved It” pulses with menacing minimalism. “Monëy So Big” rides a hypnotic horn loop that went TikTok-viral, and “Ya Ya” thumps with bass-heavy euphoria. Yeat’s voice—elastic, Auto-Tuned, and layered with ad-libs—morphs into an instrument, bending around the beats without ever feeling forced. It’s a fresh twist on Whole Lotta Red’s blueprint, though the sheer volume of tracks risks monotony.


Features


Up 2 Më is a solo showcase with one exception: SeptembersRich on “Trëndy Way.” His laid-back flow complements Yeat’s intensity, adding a mellow counterpoint to the track’s twitchy beat. The scarcity of guests keeps the focus on Yeat, a bold move that pays off—his quirky charisma carries the album. This isn’t a collab-heavy affair; it’s Yeat’s world, and he owns every corner of it.


Tracks to Watch

  • “Gët Busy” - A relentless, bell-drenched banger that explodes with Yeat’s unhinged energy, its viral hook ringing out like a war cry.

  • “Monëy So Big” - A hypnotic flex anthem, its warped horn loop and TikTok-fueled swagger making it an inescapable cultural juggernaut.

  • “Turban” - A glitchy, screeching standout that twists Yeat’s elastic flow into a wild, psychedelic fever dream.

  • “Swërved It” - A dark, pulsating gem that drips with menace, its minimalist beat and eerie vibe cementing Yeat’s rage rap throne.

Previous Works


Yeat’s journey kicked off with EPs like Deep Blue Strips (2018) and mixtapes like Alivë (2021), where he honed his glitchy, melodic trap style. Singles like “Sorry Bout That” and “Mad Bout That” built underground buzz, but Up 2 Më marks his leap forward. Compared to the looser, experimental 4L earlier in 2021, this album sharpens his sound—more cohesive, more explosive, and primed for mainstream ears. It’s Yeat graduating from SoundCloud obscurity to a rage rap frontrunner.


Summary


Up 2 Më is a 56-minute adrenaline rush—22 tracks of unrelenting energy and psychedelic trap vibes. It’s Yeat at his peak, riding the rage wave with infectious hooks and mind-bending production. The album’s length and repetitive beats can wear thin, but its highs—like “Gët Busy”—are undeniable. Up 2 Më doesn’t aim for lyrical depth; it’s a vibe-first triumph that catapults Yeat from Portland’s shadows to rap’s forefront. This is the sound of a movement taking root, raw and unpolished yet impossibly catchy.


Ratings

Originality (17/20): A vibrant, quirky spin on rage rap with Yeat’s signature vocal flair, though it echoes Carti’s shadow in places.

Production Quality (18/20): A dazzling onslaught of glitchy, bass-heavy beats that innovate within the rage lane, tempered only by slight repetition.

Lyrics and Messaging (15/20): Playful, quotable, and dripping with charisma, but lean on depth—vibe trumps substance here.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): An electrifying, addictive rush that begs to be blasted, its chaotic charm rarely letting up.

Consistency (16/20): Packed with towering peaks, yet the 22-track sprawl risks fatigue in its less gripping moments.

Overall Rating (85/100): 8.5/10

Up 2 Më isn’t a flawless debut—its length and one-note tendencies show growing pains—but it’s a bold statement from Yeat, a kid who turned TikTok snippets into a cultural moment. It’s chaotic, mindless fun elevated by stellar beats and a voice that’s impossible to ignore. In the crowded rage rap scene, this stands tall—a neon-lit Tonka truck barreling through the underground, proving Yeat’s here to stay.
