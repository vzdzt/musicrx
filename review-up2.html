UP 2 MË

Up 2 Më: Yeat’s Glitchy Ascent to Rage Rap Stardom


Dropped September 10, 2021 via Twizzy Rich and Interscope, Yeat’s Up 2 Më—a 22-track, 56-minute debut—blasted off with 150,000 first-week SoundCloud streams. Certified 2x Platinum by March 16, 2025, its 3.8 billion streams and X buzz (@36966_) keep its neon green flame burning. Fueled by BNYX and crew, it rocketed Yeat from Portland’s shadows to TikTok’s spotlight. Veazy’s 2025 take: it’s a glitchy, chaotic banger still raging strong.


The Messaging and Theme


The red-black swirl cover of Up 2 Më sets the tone—a tornado of excess and raw emotion. “Monëy So Big” flexes “I got cash, yeah, I’m up to me,” a neon green boast of wealth, while “Gët Busy” snaps “This shit get wicked,” all defiance. “Lët Ya Know” whispers vulnerability—“This could be my last song”—amid lean-fueled tales. It’s not deep; it’s a 21-year-old’s unfiltered diary of fame’s high, pure rage rap—reckless, youthful, unapologetic. In 2025, X fans (@36966_) hail it “Yeat’s chaos gospel,” its neon green vibe still pulsing through the underground.


Production and Sound


Over 20 producers—BNYX, F1LTHY, Synthetic—craft Up 2 Më, recorded in Portland, into a neon green rage rap explosion. “Turban” screeches with warped synths, “Swërved It” thumps minimalist menace, and “Monëy So Big” loops hypnotic horns into TikTok glory. Yeat’s elastic, Auto-Tuned voice—stacked with “Yeah!” ad-libs—bends around distorted 808s, riffing off Whole Lotta Red with a rawer edge. “Ya Ya” hits with bass-heavy euphoria, though 22 tracks flirt with fatigue. In 2025, its glitchy sound still fuels the rage wave.


Features


Up 2 Më is Yeat’s solo domain, with SeptembersRich on “Trëndy Way” as the lone guest—“I’m in a Benz, yeah, I’m swervin’.” His laid-back flow offsets Yeat’s twitchy energy, a neon green spark in a sparse lineup. Yeat’s quirky charisma rules, from “Gët Busy”’s bells to “BakBak”’s growls, proving he needs no backup. It’s a gutsy debut choice, keeping the focus on his neon green reign over the chaos.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Monëy So Big”, “Gët Busy”— Viral horns and sharp bells grab fast, Yeat’s neon green swagger pulling newbies into rage rap’s wild, catchy storm with instant banger vibes.

For The Dive: “Turban”, “Swërved It”— Warped synths and dark beats dive deep, Yeat’s glitchy, neon green flow weaving a chaotic spell for fans chasing the album’s raw core.

Hidden Gems: “Lët Ya Know”, “BakBak”— Quiet vulnerability and gritty snarls shine, these neon green cuts revealing Yeat’s depth under the noise, a treat for keen listeners.

Latest Obsessions: “Monëy So Big (Live 2025)”— Rolling Loud 2025’s neon green strobes ignite Yeat’s horn-heavy chaos—a viral clip Veazy can’t drop, its rage still firing up crowds.


Previous Works


Yeat kicked off with Deep Blue Strips (2018), a glitchy EP, then mixtapes like Alivë (2021) with “Sorry Bout That.” 4L (2021) honed his rage sound via “Mad Bout That,” stirring SoundCloud hype. Up 2 Më sharpens that chaos into a TikTok-ready debut, outpacing 4L’s looseness. It paved the way for 2 Alivë (2022), boosting his neon green rise. In 2025, it’s his breakout cornerstone.

Legacy & Reception

Tighter than 4L’s sprawl, its length drags but its viral highs endure. In 2025, it’s Yeat’s neon green launch, still shaking TikTok and the underground.
