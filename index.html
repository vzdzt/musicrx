Hip Hop Evolution

Underground Trend Analysis 2025


In 2025, the underground rap scene is a vibrant space where emerging artists are pushing creative boundaries, contrasting with the polished, pop-driven mainstream hip hop led by artists like Drake and Kendrick Lamar. These underground talents are known for their DIY ethos, often distributing music via platforms like SoundCloud and TikTok, and are free from major label constraints, allowing for authentic expression.

Key Points

  • Research suggests the underground rap scene in 2025 is thriving with experimental sounds like lo-fi, rage, and hyper-pop.

  • It seems likely that artists like Nine Vicious, Nettspend, Pretti Fun, and Xaviersobased are setting trends with innovative production and genre blending.

  • The evidence leans toward dark, moody themes in music videos, with artists like Dark Glo and eternalvail leading in horrorcore-inspired visuals.

  • Unexpected detail: These artists are also influencing fashion and art, creating a holistic cultural impact beyond music.

Trends and Visuals

The scene is seeing a rise in dark, moody themes, particularly in music videos, with artists like Dark Glo and eternalvail incorporating surreal, shadow-laden visuals inspired by horrorcore, enhancing the storytelling aspect of their work.


Videography and Dark Art

Dark art themes are increasingly visible in hip hop visuals, particularly in music videos. Horrorcore, a subgenre focusing on dark, violent themes, influences artists to use moody, occult, and dystopian aesthetics, enhancing the storytelling in their work. Underground rappers like Hardrock, Dark Glo, and eternalvail amplify this trend with their surreal and shadowy video styles.


Use of AI

AI is transforming hip hop, with tools generating beats, lyrics, and even simulating artists' voices, as seen in viral songs like "Heart On My Sleeve" by Ghostwriter977. This raises ethical concerns about consent and ownership, with artists like Vritra using AI to experiment with new sounds, while debates continue on its impact on human creativity. Emerging underground artists such as 454 and Project4Play are also exploring AI-driven production techniques.


Crossover with Fashion and Art

Hip hop significantly influences fashion, driving trends in streetwear and luxury collaborations, with brands like Supreme and Off-White reflecting this culture. Underground rappers like Southsidesilhouette, Diorvsyou, and Pradabagshawty are shaping the aesthetic with bold, oversized looks. Art and music intersect through album covers and music videos, with artists like Kanye West and xaviersobased embodying this convergence by blending music with visual art and fashion design.

Latest Reviews

Travis Scott - RODEO

Hip-hop soared into uncharted territory on September 4, 2015, when Travis Scott dropped Rodeo, his debut studio album under Grand Hustle and Epic Records. Building on the buzz of his 2014 mixtape Days Before Rodeo, this 14-track odyssey cemented Scott’s status as a visionary producer and polarizing rapper. As of February 19, 2025, Rodeo remains a polarizing yet iconic staple of trap music—let’s dive into its hazy, star-studded world.


The Messaging and Theme

Rodeo’s cover art—a surreal, neon-lit rodeo silhouette featuring a hyper-realistic action figure of Travis Scott, hand-sculpted by artist Dan Chung, designed by Travis Scott and visual artist David Stromberg—oozes otherworldly swagger, a perfect visual for an album that narrates Scott’s chaotic rise to fame. Narrated by T.I., the album loosely frames itself as a rebel’s journey through excess, fame, and escapism, inspired by Scott’s Houston roots and L.A. nightlife. Tracks like “90210” and “Maria I’m Drunk” paint vivid scenes of luxury and rebellion, while “Apple Pie” closes with reflective melancholy, questioning the cost of success. It’s less a linear story and more a psychedelic mood piece, capturing trap’s peak with a focus on vibe over depth.


Production and Sound

Scott’s production prowess shines, with him credited as a co-producer on every track alongside Metro Boomin, Mike Dean, WondaGurl, Sonny Digital, and others. Rodeo blends trap’s booming 808s with psychedelic synths, distorted guitars, and reverb-heavy vocals, creating a hypnotic, spacey soundscape. “Nightcrawler” pulses with dark, bass-heavy menace, while “Antidote” delivers an infectious, auto-tuned hook that became an anthem. The album’s use of auto-tune and vocal distortion—drawing from Kid Cudi and Kanye West—adds a dreamy, disorienting layer, prioritizing atmosphere over precision.


Features

Rodeo boasts a star-studded lineup of guests, amplifying its cultural impact. Future, 2 Chainz, Juicy J, The Weeknd, Swae Lee, Chief Keef, Kanye West, Justin Bieber, Young Thug, Quavo, Toro y Moi, and Schoolboy Q bring diverse energy, often stealing the spotlight. The Weeknd’s haunting vocals on “Pray 4 Love” and Bieber’s unexpected turn on “Maria I’m Drunk” are highlights, while Quavo’s verse on “Oh My Dis Side” adds a trap punch. These features elevate Scott’s vision but sometimes overshadow his own rapping, raising questions about his role as a rapper versus curator.


Tracks to Watch

  • “90210” - A cinematic opener blending haunting piano and Scott’s auto-tuned storytelling.

  • “Antidote” - The breakout hit, a hypnotic trap banger with a sticky hook.

  • “Maria I’m Drunk” (feat. Justin Bieber & Young Thug) - A wild, star-powered party anthem.

  • “Nightcrawler” (feat. Swae Lee & Chief Keef) - A dark, bass-heavy standout forlate-night vibes.

Previous Works

Before Rodeo, Travis Scott built his name with mixtapes like Owl Pharaoh (2013) and Days Before Rodeo (2014), showcasing his production skills and auto-tuned flow. Those projects laid the groundwork for Rodeo’s psychedelic trap sound, drawing from Houston’s chopped-and-screwed tradition and Scott’s collaborations with artists like T.I. and Kanye West. Rodeo marks his evolution from mixtape phenom to major-label star, refining the hazy aesthetic that would later define Astroworld.


Summary

Rodeo is a 65-minute thrill ride that thrives on production and atmosphere, even if its lyrical depth falters. Scott’s visionary beats and all-star features create a psychedelic trap masterpiece, but the album’s length and Scott’s uneven rapping—often criticized as monotonous orsubstance-lacking—can wear thin. It’s not perfect, but its cultural impact is undeniable, spawning hits like “Antidote” and influencing trap’s evolution. As of February 19, 2025, Rodeo remains a fan favorite and a polarizing classic, less about complexity and more about Scott’s bold, hazy vision.


Ratings

Originality (17/20): A fresh take on trap, blending psychedelic elements with Houston’s roots.

Production Quality (19/20): Stellar beats by Scott and co-producers are the album’s backbone.

Lyrics and Messaging (12/20): Lyrical content leans heavily on party themes, lacking depth.

Enjoyment Factor (18/20): Addictive for fans of trap’s vibe, though inconsistent pacing may tire some.

Consistency (15/20): Strong front-to-back but drags in spots, especially toward the end.

Overall Rating (81/100): 8.1/10

Rodeo isn’t flawless, but it’s a defining moment in trap music, showcasing Scott’s production genius and star power. Love it for its highs, forgive its lows, and blast it loud—this album proves that sometimes, a killer beat and vibe can carry the day, even if the words don’t always match.

Aubrey Graham - DRAKE

From his early days as Jimmy Brooks on Degrassi to becoming one of the most streamed artists of all time, Aubrey Drake Graham has redefined what it means to be a hip-hop superstar in the digital age.


Comprehensive Analysis

Drake, born Aubrey Drake Graham on October 24, 1986, in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, is a Canadian rapper, singer, songwriter, and producer who has become one of the most influential figures in contemporary music. His career, spanning nearly two decades since his debut in 2006, is characterized by chart dominance, record-breaking streaming numbers, and a significant cultural impact.



Rise to Fame

Drake's journey began with his breakthrough mixtape So Far Gone in 2009, which bridged hip-hop with R&B in ways that would become his signature sound. The success of tracks like "Best I Ever Had" caught the attention of Lil Wayne, leading to his signing with Young Money Entertainment. His debut album Thank Me Later (2010) solidified his place in the industry, debuting at number one on the Billboard 200.


Career Milestones

  • 2011: Released the critically acclaimed Take Care, which won Grammy for Best Rap Album

  • 2015: Released surprise mixtape If You're Reading This It's Too Late, breaking streaming records

  • 2016: Views became Drake's highest-selling album, with "One Dance" becoming his first solo #1 hit

  • 2018: Scorpion broke streaming records with hits like "God's Plan" and "In My Feelings"

  • 2023: Surpassed 75 billion streams across all platforms, making him one of music's most-streamed artists

  • The Boy has won numerous awards, including four Grammy Awards, 29 Billboard Music Awards, and 17 American Music Awards.

  • Drake's impact on music and popular culture is undeniable, and he continues to be one of the most popular artists in the world.

World and Industry Records

  • Most #1 albums on Billboard 200: 13
  • Most charted songs: 358
  • Most top 10 hits: 80
  • Most consecutive weeks on chart: 431
  • 13 Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles

Achievements and Awards

Drake has won 5 Grammy Awards from 51 nominations, 6 American Music Awards, and 29 Billboard Music Awards from 39 nods. Named Artist of the Decade by Billboard in 2021, he also received IFPI Global Recording Artist of the Year in 2016 and 2018.

Longevity and Records Sold

Album Sales: Over 20 million units in the US, 170 million records worldwide.

Single Sales: Over 244 million digital units. Streaming: Over 75 billion streams on Spotify.



Musical Style & Impact

Drake's ability to seamlessly blend rap, R&B, pop, and dancehall has created a sound that's distinctly his own yet widely imitated. His emotional vulnerability in tracks like "Marvin's Room" and "Jungle" opened doors for a new generation of introspective hip-hop artists. Beyond music, his OVO brand has expanded into fashion, radio, and business ventures, establishing him as a cultural entrepreneur.


Essential Listening

For Newcomers: "Hotline Bling," "God's Plan," "Started From The Bottom," "One Dance"

For The Dive: "Marvin's Room," "Jungle," "Pound Cake," "Do Not Disturb"

Hidden Gems: "Paris Morton Music," "Days In The East," "Club Paradise," "4PM in Calabasas"



Legacy

As Drake continues to evolve, his influence on music, language, and pop culture remains undeniable. He's transformed from a child actor to a global superstar whose releases are cultural events. Whether through meme-worthy dance moves or quotable lyrics, Drake has mastered the art of cultural relevance in an ever-changing digital landscape. His legacy as one of hip-hop's most commercially successful and culturally impactful artists is already secured, regardless of where his artistic journey takes him next.
