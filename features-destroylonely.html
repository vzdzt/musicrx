DESTROY LONELY

The Boundary-Pushing Opium Visionary


With his ethereal production choices and distinctive vocals, Destroy Lonely has established himself as one of the most innovative artists under Playboi Carti's Opium label, pushing the boundaries of contemporary rap with a sound that's both experimental and hypnotic.


Rise to Fame


Born Bobby Sandimanie III on July 8, 1999, in Atlanta, Georgia, Destroy Lonely began releasing music on SoundCloud in 2015. His early tracks caught the attention of underground rap fans with their unique blend of cloud rap and punk influences. By 2021, his growing buzz led to signing with Playboi Carti's Opium label. Though he had gained a significant underground following with projects like <3 and Overseas, it was his 2022 major label debut No Stylist that thrust him into the wider hip-hop conversation, followed by 2023's If Looks Could Kill, which further cemented his distinctive artistic vision.


Career Milestones

  • 2018: Released breakout singles "Bane" and "Kross Kountry"

  • 2020: Released underground hit mixtape <3

  • 2021: Signed to Playboi Carti's Opium label

  • 2022: Released major label debut No Stylist, which reached #133 on the Billboard 200

  • 2023: Released sophomore album If Looks Could Kill, debuting at #16 on the Billboard 200

Musical Style & Impact


Destroy Lonely's music exists at the intersection of cloud rap, trap, and punk aesthetics. His vocals—often layered with reverb and delivered with an effortless melodic flow—float above atmospheric, spacey production that incorporates unconventional sounds and textures. Tracks like "NOSTYLIST", "VETERAN", and "ILLUSION" showcase his ability to create immersive soundscapes that push genre boundaries. His fashion-forward image and mysterious persona have further cemented his status as a trendsetter in underground hip-hop circles.


Essential Listening

For Newcomers: "NOSTYLIST," "VETERAN," "ILLUSION," "CRIMES"

For The Dive: "Dover Street Market," "if looks could kill," "TESTING ME," "HLDMYHND"

Hidden Gems: "TURNINUP," "DANGEROUS," "KROSS KOUNTRY," "Bane"


Legacy


Though still in the early stages of his career, Destroy Lonely is already carving out a distinct lane in hip-hop's avant-garde space. As a key member of Playboi Carti's Opium roster alongside Ken Carson, he represents a new wave of artists blending internet-based aesthetics with experimental production techniques. His influence extends beyond music into fashion and visual art, creating a holistic artistic vision that resonates particularly with younger listeners. As he continues to evolve, Lonely's willingness to push creative boundaries positions him as an artist who could significantly impact hip-hop's sonic landscape in the coming years.
