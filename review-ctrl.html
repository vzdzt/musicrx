Introduction

Released June 9 2017 via Top Dawg Entertainment and RCA Records SZA’s Ctrl a 14 track 49 minute debut landed at number 3 on the Billboard 200 with 60000 equivalent album units. Certified 3x Platinum by August 2022 its 5.1 billion streams and 400 weeks on the chart by February 2025 cement its legacy. With producers like Frank Dukes and Carter Lang it redefined R&B blending neo soul and raw emotion. In 2025 it remains a cultural touchstone.

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ctrl_%28SZA_album%29)[](https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/sza-first-number-one-album-billboard-200-chart-sos-1235189465/)

Messaging and Theme

Ctrl’s forest green cover hints at vulnerability a confessional diary of love and self doubt. “Love Galore” sighs “Why you bother me when you know you don’t want me” raw heartbreak while “The Weekend” explores messy shared love “You be texting back you say I’m not your type.” “Supermodel” admits “I’m only human and I’m helpless” tackling insecurity. SZA’s lyrics weave jealousy desire and empowerment into a 20 something’s emotional chaos. In 2025 its honest purple hued storytelling still resonates deeply.

[](https://sza.fandom.com/wiki/Ctrl)

Production

Carter Lang Frank Dukes ThankGod4Cody and others shape Ctrl into a neo soul R&B gem recorded at TDE Red Room. “Broken Clocks” floats with dreamy synths “Drew Barrymore” hums lo fi warmth and “Doves in the Wind” blends jazzy keys with hip hop grit. SZA’s airy vocals dance over minimal beats pulling from 80s soul and 40s jazz for inspiration. The analog process unplugging wires for texture gives it a timeless purple glow. In 2025 its cohesive sound still feels fresh.

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ctrl_%28SZA_album%29)[](https://sza.fandom.com/wiki/Ctrl)

Features

Ctrl shines with sparse but potent guests. Travis Scott on “Love Galore” adds slick charm “Done with these niggas I don’t love these niggas” while Kendrick Lamar on “Doves in the Wind” drops poetic bars “Real niggas do not deserve pussy.” Isaiah Rashad and James Fauntleroy bring soulful depth to “Pretty Little Birds” and “Wavy”. SZA’s voice remains the star her purple tinged emotion carrying the album’s heart.

Previous Works

SZA debuted with See.SZA.Run 2012 a self released EP followed by S 2013 catching Top Dawg Entertainment’s ear. Her third EP Z 2014 hit Billboard 200 at number 39 with “Child’s Play” featuring Chance the Rapper. Ctrl built on Z’s lo fi soul sharpening it into a cohesive neo soul masterpiece. It laid the foundation for SOS 2022 which topped charts. In 2025 Ctrl remains her defining purple hued origin.

Legacy & Reception

Pitchfork gave Ctrl an 8.4/10 praising its “radical honesty” while fans on X in 2025 call it a “time capsule of emotions.” With 3x Platinum status 5.1 billion streams and 400 weeks on the Billboard 200 by February 2025 it’s a neo soul cornerstone. Ranked 472 on Rolling Stone’s 500 Greatest Albums it outshines Z’s rawness. Its purple glow still shapes R&B’s emotional landscape.

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ctrl_%28SZA_album%29)[](https://www.therichest.com/sza-net-worth/)

Awards Recognition

Ctrl earned four Grammy nominations in 2018 including Best Urban Contemporary Album and Best New Artist for SZA. Its singles “Love Galore” and “The Weekend” also nabbed nods. While it missed wins its 3x Platinum status and 5.1 billion streams by 2025 prove its purple hued grip on R&B’s heart.
