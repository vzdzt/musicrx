NOTHING WAS THE SAME

DRAKE - NWTS


On September 24, 2013, Drake released Nothing Was the Same, a 13-track masterpiece that debuted at #1 on the Billboard 200, selling nearly 700,000 copies in its first week. Following the breakout success of Take Care, this album solidified Drake as rap’s emotional kingpin. Here’s a deep dive into this moody, polished classic:


Messaging and Theme

The album’s cover—a child Drake staring at his adult self—sets the tone: a reflective journey through fame, love, and identity. Nothing Was the Same is Drake at his most introspective, balancing braggadocio with vulnerability. Tracks like “Tuscan Leather” and “Started From the Bottom” flex his rise, while “From Time” and “Too Much” peel back layers of regret and family strain. It’s not a narrative like Kendrick’s work but a vibe-driven memoir—Drake wrestling with success, relationships, and Toronto’s ghosts. This is his crown, worn with both pride and melancholy.


Production and Sound

Led by Noah “40” Shebib, with contributions from Boi-1da, Mike Zombie, and Nineteen85, Nothing Was the Same is a sonic haze of lush beats and icy ambiance. “Hold On, We’re Going Home” glides with ‘80s synth-pop sheen, while “Worst Behavior” bangs with menacing trap energy. “Tuscan Leather” weaves a three-part suite with Whitney Houston samples, and “Furthest Thing” drifts on dreamy keys. Drake’s voice—smooth, melodic, and occasionally sharp—floats over these soundscapes, blending rap with R&B in a way that’s both cinematic and intimate. It’s a refined evolution of Take Care’s sprawl.


Features

Nothing Was the Same keeps features lean, letting Drake shine. Jhené Aiko adds soulful depth to “From Time,” while Majid Jordan co-stars on “Hold On, We’re Going Home.” Jay-Z brings gravitas to “Pound Cake/Paris Morton Music 2,” and Sampha haunts “Too Much.” These guests don’t dominate—they’re accents to Drake’s vision, enhancing the album’s emotional palette without breaking its focus.


Tracks to Watch

  • "Tuscan Leather" - A sprawling, confident opener that sets the tone.
  • "Started From the Bottom" - A gritty, anthemic flex of Drake’s journey.
  • "Hold On, We’re Going Home" - A timeless, silky love song.
  • "From Time" - A tender, reflective gem with raw emotion.

Previous Works

Drake’s ascent began with So Far Gone (2009), followed by Thank Me Later (2010), which introduced his rap-R&B blend. Take Care (2011) perfected that formula with moody sprawl. Nothing Was the Same tightens Take Care’s loose ends, trading some of its experimentation for focus and polish. Where Take Care was a breakthrough, this album is a refinement—Drake honing his sound into a sleek, introspective statement.


Summary

Nothing Was the Same is a 59-minute mood piece—13 tracks of lush production and raw introspection. It’s Drake at his peak, crafting a cohesive, emotionally charged opus that balances hits with depth. Tracks like “Connect” can feel understated, but the highs—like “Tuscan Leather”—are towering. This isn’t just an album—it’s a milestone, cementing Drake’s reign as rap’s introspective titan. It’s sleek, soulful, and timeless, a testament to his evolution.


Ratings

Originality (18/20): A refined take on Drake’s sound, though it builds on past work.

Production Quality (20/20): Immaculate—lush, cohesive, and perfectly crafted.

Lyrics and Messaging (19/20): Poignant and clever, with rare dips in depth.

Enjoyment Factor (19/20): A captivating, replayable journey with broad appeal.

Consistency (18/20): Near-flawless, with a few tracks less gripping.

Overall Rating (94/100): 9.4/10

Nothing Was the Same isn’t Drake’s most experimental or raw—it’s his most polished, a snapshot of an artist in command.
