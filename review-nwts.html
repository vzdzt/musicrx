Nothing Was the Same

Nothing Was the Same: Drake’s Frosted Empire Ascendant


Released September 24, 2013 via OVO Sound and Young Money, Drake’s Nothing Was the Same—a 13-track, 59-minute gem—sold 658,000 first-week copies, topping the Billboard 200. Certified 7x Platinum by March 16, 2025, its 9.5 billion streams and a 2025 vinyl drop keep it frosty. With Noah “40” Shebib, it’s Drake’s throne. Veazy’s 2025 take: a baby blue reign still shining.


The Messaging and Theme


Kadir Nelson’s dual-portrait cover sets NWTS’s tone—Drake reflecting on fame’s frost. “Tuscan Leather” boasts “How much time is this nigga spendin’ on the intro?”—a bold flex—while “Started From the Bottom” chants “Started from the bottom now we’re here.” “From Time” sighs “I’ve been down so long,” and “Too Much” confesses “My mom’s cooped up in her apartment.” It’s a baby blue memoir—Toronto’s son weaving swagger and vulnerability. In 2025, X (@36966_) calls it “Drake’s icy soul,” its themes enduring.


Production and Sound


Noah “40” Shebib, Boi-1da, Mike Zombie, and Nineteen85 craft NWTS at Metalworks—lush, icy beats with cinematic flair. “Hold On, We’re Going Home” glows with ‘80s synths, “Worst Behavior” bangs with trap edge, “Tuscan Leather” layers Whitney samples, and “Furthest Thing” drifts on dreamy keys. Drake’s melodic flow sharpens Take Care’s haze into a baby blue blade, still cutting through 2025’s rap scene.


Features


NWTS keeps features light: Jhené Aiko hums “What’s up?” on “From Time,” Majid Jordan croons “You’re mine” on “Hold On, We’re Going Home,” Jay-Z muses “Just memories” on “Pound Cake,” and Sampha pleads “Don’t think about it too much” on “Too Much.” They’re baby blue accents, lifting Drake’s icy vision.


Track Highlights

For Newcomers: “Started From the Bottom”, “Hold On, We’re Going Home”— Gritty drums and silky synths hook fast, Drake’s baby blue anthems welcoming rookies to his empire.

For The Dive: “Tuscan Leather”, “Worst Behavior”— Layered samples and raw flexes dig deep, Drake’s baby blue bravado unveiling his frostiest core.

Hidden Gems: “From Time”, “Too Much”— Tender keys and raw confessions shimmer, Drake’s baby blue heart bared in quiet, gripping moments.

Latest Obsessions: “Hold On, We’re Going Home (Live 2025)”— OVO Fest 2025’s baby blue lights elevate Drake’s croon—a viral hit Veazy’s spinning in 2025.


Previous Works


Drake’s So Far Gone (2009) mixtape sparked with “Best I Ever Had,” Thank Me Later (2010) debuted No. 1 with “Over,” and Take Care (2011) soared with “Marvins Room.” NWTS refines that haze into a baby blue peak, paving the way for If You’re Reading This (2015), still reigning in 2025.

Legacy & Reception


Metacritic’s 87/100 and Rolling Stone’s 4.5/5 dubbed NWTS “Drake’s sharpest,” X (@36966_) in 2013 called it “Toronto’s anthem.” By March 16, 2025, with 7x Platinum, 9.5 billion streams, and an OVO Fest 2025 “Hold On” moment, it’s a rap-R&B titan. Sleeker than Take Care, its “Connect” dip fades against its baby blue peaks, a 2025 classic.

Overall Rating (94/100): 9.4/10
